UNILIFE OS — AI WORKSPACE ATTACHMENTS & UNIBOT SCOPE UPDATE

Changes:
- Added a Gemini-inspired + attachment menu to the AI Study Hub composer.
- Added multi-file selection (up to 5 files, max 8 MB each).
- PDF files are parsed in the browser with the existing PDF.js dependency and their extracted text is included in the AI prompt.
- TXT files are read locally and included in the AI prompt.

- UniBot is now rendered only on the Dashboard route; it is hidden and cleared on all other routes.
- Service worker cache version bumped to v13.
